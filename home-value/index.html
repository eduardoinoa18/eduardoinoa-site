Your analysis

What's in your market analysis.

  • Comparable sales. Similar homes sold recently near yours, with price, date and the differences that matter.
  • Active competition. What's for sale today: the homes buyers will see alongside yours.
  • Price per square foot. How your home compares by size with nearby sales and listings.
  • Condition adjustments. Kitchen, baths, roof, systems, finished basement and parking, and what each does to price.

Online estimates

Why online estimates can miss.

Automated models are a useful starting point. But they work from public records, not from your home.

  1. They can't see inside

    A renovated kitchen, a finished basement or a new roof rarely shows up in public records.

  2. Records lag

    Recent updates, added units or changes in use take time to appear, if they appear at all.

  3. Few sales, wider error

    In smaller towns, or where homes differ a lot, an automated model has less to compare against.

  4. The street matters

    Two similar homes a few blocks apart can sell for different prices because of the street, the lot or the view.

If an online estimate disagrees with mine, that's exactly where we should talk.

Questions

Answered plainly.

Is this an appraisal?

No. A comparative market analysis is my opinion of price as a licensed agent, built from comparable sales and active competition. An appraisal is done by a licensed appraiser, usually for a lender.

Is the figure shown my home's value?

No. It's Zillow's typical home value for your town or county: an area figure. Your home's value depends on its size, condition, updates and street, and that is what the analysis measures.

Does it cost anything, or commit me to selling?

There's no cost and no obligation. Plenty of people ask for one to refinance, to plan ahead, or simply out of curiosity.

How long does it take?

I reply personally, usually the same day. The written analysis depends on how many comparable sales there are and whether we do a walkthrough.

Do you need to see the inside?

It isn't required, but it helps. Updates, condition and layout don't show up in public records; a short walkthrough makes the range more accurate.

Can I get it in Spanish?

Yes. The conversation and the written analysis can be entirely in Spanish.
