Questions

Answered plainly.

Who is Eduardo Inoa?
Eduardo Inoa is a bilingual REALTOR® with The Fermin Group at Century 21 North East, licensed in Massachusetts and New Hampshire since 2021. He has guided clients through more than 35 transactions and over $20 million in sales across the Merrimack Valley and Southern New Hampshire. He is a graduate of Northern Essex Community College and a Massachusetts Notary Public.
What does it cost to work with you as a buyer?
Our fee is in writing before we tour, and in many transactions the seller covers some or all of it. When they don't, we agree up front how any difference is handled. Never a surprise at closing.
What will selling actually cost?
My listing fee (negotiable), any buyer-agent compensation you choose to offer, state transfer stamps, attorney and prorations. The net proceeds tool shows every line, and I will hand you a written net sheet.
Do you work with investors?
Yes — multi-family, house hacks, BRRRR, flips and wholesale. The toolkit is the same analysis we use together, exported as a PDF report.
Can I sell and buy at the same time?
Yes. Sell first (with a flexible close or rent-back), buy first, a sale-contingent offer, or a bridge loan. We plan both closings so you move once, and I represent you in both transactions.
What areas do you serve?
The Merrimack Valley in Massachusetts and Southern New Hampshire, from our office at 130 Parker St, Lawrence, MA. If your town is outside that area, I will connect you with an agent I trust.
¿Trabaja en español?
Absolutely. The whole process — documents, negotiation and signings — can be entirely in Spanish.
