The short version

Pelham, briefly

Pelham sits directly north of Dracut and Methuen, with Tyngsborough to the south-west, Hudson to the west and Windham and Salem to the east. There is no interstate in town: NH Route 38 along Bridge Street is its commercial hub, and I-93 is about six miles away in Salem.

For buyers it offers New Hampshire living within a few minutes of Massachusetts — Lowell and Methuen are just over the line.

Housing

What the housing stock looks like

Pelham is mostly single-family: colonials, capes and ranches, many on larger and often wooded lots, along with newer construction.

Private wells and septic systems are common, so we confirm both before an offer. On taxes: New Hampshire has no general sales tax and no state income tax on wages, but relies more on local property taxes — compare the actual tax bill on any home. If you work in Massachusetts, Massachusetts generally taxes the wages you earn there.

Getting around

Getting to and from Pelham

Check the MBTA and MassDOT or NHDOT for current schedules and roadwork.

  • By car

    NH Route 38 (Bridge Street), Route 111A and Route 128 — Mammoth Road, which runs from Lowell north toward Hooksett — cross the town. I-93 is about six miles away in Salem, and I-495 about nine miles south in Massachusetts.

  • By train

    There is no passenger rail in Pelham. The closest commuter rail stations are across the line in Massachusetts, in Lowell and Lawrence.

  • Across the line

    Dracut, Methuen and Tyngsborough, Massachusetts, border Pelham to the south.

Schools

Schools in Pelham

Pelham’s public schools are run by the Pelham School District. For school assignments, enrollment and programs, go straight to the district — it is the only source I point clients to.

I don't rank or rate schools; the district and the state are the sources to trust.

Good to know

A few things about Pelham

  1. Beaver Brook

    Nearly all of the town drains into Beaver Brook, which flows south to the Merrimack in Lowell.

  2. Route 38

    Bridge Street is the town’s commercial hub.

  3. Incorporated in 1746

    Pelham was split from Old Dunstable in the 1740s and incorporated in 1746.

Questions

Answered plainly

Does New Hampshire have a sales tax or income tax?

New Hampshire has no general sales tax and no state income tax on wages, and its interest-and-dividends tax was repealed for tax periods beginning in 2025. Property-tax rates are generally higher than in many nearby Massachusetts towns, so always compare the actual tax bill. If you work in Massachusetts, ask a tax professional how your wages are taxed.

How far is Pelham from I-93 and I-495?

I-93 is about six miles from the town center, in Salem, and I-495 is about nine miles south in Massachusetts.

What kinds of homes are in Pelham?

Mostly single-family colonials, capes and ranches, many on larger wooded lots, along with newer construction.
